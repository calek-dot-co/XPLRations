Peter's 14th Defence Bunker

Somewhere, Estonia

Part of Coastal Defence Battery No. 14 within the Peter the Great's Sea Fortress, this bunker is one of many that looks towards the Bay of Tallinn. Tucked into the north-east corner of the bunker complex, beneath the shady trees in this nature reserve, the command post bunkers offers access to a rooftop observation platform alongside several decaying rooms, now permanently flooded with water.
