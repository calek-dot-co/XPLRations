Overgrown Island Cemetery

Somewhere, Estonia

Fenced off by old steel tracks from a dismantled railway, an overgrown cemetery rests at the centre of this small Baltic island. During my visit, rusting mid-19th-century cast-iron crosses and moss-covered mounds were backlit by dappled light from the late afternoon sun.

With the final burials occurring here in the early 20th century, this cemetery's residents remain undisturbed except for the passing of time and the growth of nature.
