The Artist's Unfinished Castle

Somewhere, Poland

Construction began in 1979 on a grand estate for a local sculptor named Piotr. The faux-castle was designed to be full of symbolism: 12 turrets, one for each month; 52 rooms, one for each week; 365 windows, one for each day in a year; and a ballroom and indoor swimming pool just for a bit of fun. Unfortunately, the enormous castle was abandoned before it was ever completed due to lack of funds and permits.
