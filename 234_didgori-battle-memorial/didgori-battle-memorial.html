Didgori Battle Memorial

Didgori, Georgia

Giant swords in the ground memorialise the Battle of Didgori. During this battle, fought in the early 1100s in Didgori's narrow valleys, King David IV’s Georgian Christian forces defeated Ilghazi's Seljuk Muslim forces. This turning point in the war allowed David IV to reclaim Tbilisi, restore it as the capital, and mark the onset of the medieval Georgian Golden Age.
