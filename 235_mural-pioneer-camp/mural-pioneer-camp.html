Mural Pioneer Camp

Somewhere, Georgia

Modern life is gradually coming to the countryside of Georgia. It arrives in the form of new roads, international visitors, and the economic impact of tourism. But the future is not evenly distributed; it arrives quickly in some places, slowly in others, and sometimes never for places that have been forgotten. Once a pioneer camp that taught the ideals of the Soviet way of life, the future's progress has stopped just at the gate of this location.

Rusting signs welcome the occasional visitor before they slip past the fence and into the past. Across the grounds of this former camp you'll find an admin building, toilet block, and dormitories, but the highlight has to be the former cafeteria with its decaying fit-out and surprisingly beautiful wall mural. Spread across 12 canvases, the inkblot artwork depicts the mountains and valleys of Georgia and the people of the countryside who lived in the country's progression from the past to today.

Even more than 30 years after the fall of communism in Georgia, this former state-run camp's beauty is still fully on display. I'm left wondering what life would have been like growing up holidaying at a place like this.
