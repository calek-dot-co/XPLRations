Inland U-boat Loch

Somewhere, Poland

Roughly 120 kilometers inland from the Baltic Sea, several massive concrete structures interrupt a canal running through the Polish forest. It was rumoured, but never proven, that these concrete monsters was built to transport U-boats from the coast to the Nazi bunker complex in Mauerwald.

Functioning as a lock, this raised channel was once fitted with a series of gates and pumps, built to help float boats to higher elevations. This loch was part of infrastructure on the man-made Masurian Canal that connected East Prussia (now Russia's Kaliningrad Oblast) and Poland's Great Masurian Lakes.

Although the gates and pumps are long gone, the prominent recess where the Nazi eagle and swastika emblem hung remains unchanged. Recent history has tried to repurpose the site into an obstacle course, but it seems that the monolithic steel-reinforced concrete has ultimately outlasted both family-friendly adventures and dreams of fascist world domination.
